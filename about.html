About Myriam

M Skin Couture is Myriam’s space for signature glow and skin rejuvenation — treatments that blend advanced technology with a calm, couture-level ritual.

Her approach is results-focused and skin-kind: improving clarity, texture, and hydration while protecting the barrier and keeping downtime to a minimum wherever possible.

From glass-skin dermaplaning and LED renewal to PRX-T33, medical needling, Hydrafacial, and IPL, every protocol is chosen to help skin look fresher, clearer, and more radiant — without a harsh clinical feel.

Whether you are preparing for an event, rebuilding glow after dullness, or working on pigmentation and redness over a course of visits, Myriam will guide you toward the treatment that fits your skin and your rhythm.
